# Decisions reserved for future community co-design

MindCommons is an independent technical prototype. The developer cannot establish community authority by writing software or by creating fictional demo roles. The following decisions must remain open until an authorized process is agreed with the host and people affected. Selection for a fellowship would not itself substitute for that process.

## Questions to decide together

- Which people and groups are affected, and who should be invited to shape the process?
- What information, if any, is in scope, and what categories should remain excluded?
- Which research purposes, model types, outputs, audiences and downstream uses are acceptable or prohibited?
- What must be explained for an individual to make a meaningful choice, in which languages and formats?
- How do an individual's refusal or withdrawal and a collective decision interact?
- Who may propose, review, approve, reject, pause and revisit a policy? How are representatives selected and held accountable?
- How can people object, ask questions, appeal, seek support or report harm without retaliation?
- What does a valid decision process require beyond a simple majority, if voting is used at all?
- What benefits matter, who accepts responsibility, what evidence is appropriate, and how can terms be renegotiated or challenged?
- What data retention, deletion, incident response and model-provider limits are acceptable?
- What ethics, legal, privacy, safeguarding and security approvals are required before a pilot?

## Process commitments for the proposed fellowship

First establish the relationship, authorization and safeguards with the host. Then agree a supported, accessible way to involve young people, people with lived experience and community health workers. Give participants a real opportunity to change or reject technical assumptions. Document differing views where safe and authorized. Use synthetic data until specific human-subject activities and data have been separately approved. Define stop conditions and a no-pilot alternative before any pilot is considered.

The initial demo's role switcher, one fictional representative, single policy proposal and synthetic consent buttons are test scaffolding only. They do not answer these questions. No community feedback, SRHIN endorsement, or pilot approval is claimed.

# Mozilla RFP alignment and evidence map

The 2027–2028 RFP identifies agency, open infrastructure (“Commons”), and changing economic conditions as core questions, asks whether affected people shape the work, whether it can be built, and asks applicants to show an inspectable artifact. SRHIN's Nigeria brief describes community-owned governance, consent, privacy and benefit-sharing, with a pilot involving young people, people with lived experience and community health workers. See the official [RFP PDF](https://assets.mofoprod.net/network/documents/PUBLIC_2027_2028_Fellowship_RFP-2.pdf), especially its printed page 7, and [call page](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/fellowship/2027-call-for-applications/).

# Mozilla RFP alignment and evidence map

The 2027–2028 RFP asks whether affected people shape the work, whether the applicant can build it, and whether the project can change who holds power and benefits from emerging technology. The SRHIN Nigeria brief prioritizes community-owned governance, consent, privacy, benefit-sharing, and eventual pilot testing with young people, people with lived experience, and community health workers. See the official [current RFP PDF](https://assets.mofoprod.net/network/documents/PUBLIC_2027_2028_Fellowship_RFP-3.pdf), especially the SRHIN challenge, and [call page](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/fellowship/2027-call-for-applications/).

The current Letter of Intent form's Builder Profile field requests a public artifact link and allows up to 200 words of explanation. It does not specify a video upload or file-size limit. MindCommons will link to the MP4 from the public site; the applicant may replace that link with a YouTube URL later.

MindCommons is a proposed project, not a Mozilla award, an SRHIN partnership, or a community-owned cooperative today.

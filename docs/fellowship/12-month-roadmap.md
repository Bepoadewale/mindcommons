# Proposed 12-month roadmap

This is a draft proposal for June 2027–May 2028, subject to refinement with any selected host and affected communities. It is not a commitment by SRHIN or Mozilla.

| Quarter | Work | Public outputs and decision gates |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: Listen and scope | Host and participant discovery; map governance, safeguarding, language, accessibility and research questions; privacy/legal/ethics planning; review architecture with community advisors. | Co-designed scope and governance options; publish methods and open technical backlog. No participant data collection before approvals. |
| Q2: Co-design and build | Prototype governance and consent flows with compensated advisors; refine threat model and consent language; build policy contracts and accessible researcher/community views. | Versioned prototype, tests, design rationale, risk register; participant review determines whether to proceed. |
| Q3: Controlled pilot preparation | Obtain appropriate approvals; train facilitators; conduct synthetic dry runs; only if approved, begin a bounded pilot with synthetic or explicitly approved data and community-controlled safeguards. | Published protocol and operational controls; pause if consent, safety, or hosting terms are unclear. |
| Q4: Evaluate and maintain | Evaluate agency, comprehension, usefulness, withdrawals, appeals and benefit commitments; remediate; publish code, documentation and findings; agree maintenance and governance stewardship. | Public evaluation, roadmap, maintainer and sustainability plan. No sensitive data publication. |

## Conditions and risks

Timeline depends on host collaboration, participant availability, ethics review, lawful data arrangements, safeguarding and accessible language support. If real-data work is not safe or approved, complete a synthetic pilot and governance evaluation instead. Success is not defined by model accuracy alone; community agency and accountability must be evaluated by participants.

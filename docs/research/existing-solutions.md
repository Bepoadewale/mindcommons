This is a research snapshot, not a claim of novelty. Updated 2026-11-17 after implementation of the portable Community AI Agreement.

## Relevant Nigerian community-health context

SRHIN's public article on its BraveHeart Innovation describes community leaders trained to recognize anxiety and depression and provide psychosocial support and mental-health education to young people in two IDP camps. This is useful local context for designing with community health workers and young people. It is not a MindCommons pilot, endorsement, partnership, or evidence that SRHIN has agreed to host this project. Source: <https://srhin.org/breaking-the-silence-how-braveheart-innovation-is-bringing-mental-health-closer-to-young-people-in-abujas-idp-camps/>.

## What MindCommons proposes to add

An open, independently deployable reference agreement and evaluator linking a versioned community rule, separate individual permissions, bounded job and model restrictions, authorization, output-release recheck, provenance, and benefit obligations. MindCommons uses the evaluator; a separate minimal Worker example demonstrates how another participating service can consume the same agreement. This is an interoperability experiment with a concrete scope, not a claim of novelty or a universal enforcement protocol. It cannot control unrestricted copies of datasets or model weights, services that ignore the agreement, or downstream use of already released outputs. The prototype does not prove legal compliance, consent validity, model unlearning, privacy-preserving computation, representative governance, or adoption.

## Further validation needed

Test whether the agreement schema is understandable and useful to independent researchers and implementers, compare it with existing licences and data-governance standards, and seek input from Nigeria-based community health practitioners. Do not imply that this separate example is deployed or interoperates with Mozilla Data Collective, PySyft, or Flower. Seek no data or endorsement from SRHIN unless a real relationship is established. Co-design authority, appeals, safety, language, compensation, and accountability arrangements before any pilot.

# Mozilla RFP alignment and evidence map

The official [2027–2028 RFP PDF](https://assets.mofoprod.net/network/documents/PUBLIC_2027_2028_Fellowship_RFP-3.pdf) provides the source passages below. The short quoted fragments are drawn from its cross-fellowship criteria, SRHIN host challenge, and evaluation questions; together they reproduce 24 words from the RFP. All other entries are paraphrases. The [call page](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-do/grantmaking/fellowship/2027-call-for-applications/) provides program context.

The Letter of Intent Builder Profile asks for a public link to an artifact and allows up to 200 words of explanation. The RFP form contains no video-file upload field or video-size limit; the current public MP4 link is suitable as an artifact URL. The applicant may replace it with a YouTube URL later. See the Builder Profile questions in the [official RFP PDF](https://assets.mofoprod.net/network/documents/PUBLIC_2027_2028_Fellowship_RFP-3.pdf).

MindCommons is a proposed project, not a Mozilla award, an SRHIN partnership, or a community-owned cooperative today.

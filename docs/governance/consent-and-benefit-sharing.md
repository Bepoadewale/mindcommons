# Consent and benefit sharing

Collective rules and individual permission are separate inputs. An otherwise approved community request must be denied if an individual's relevant permission is absent, expired, withdrawn or restricted. Collective approval cannot override an individual's refusal or legal rights.

The demo stores fictional purpose, status, expiry and version facts, plus a session-scoped history for each recorded permission transition. Each entry records the resulting state, action, actor, timestamp and correlation identifier. A session is limited to 50 versions; reset and expiry delete the synthetic session and its history. Existing sessions are backfilled with their current state when the migration runs; their earlier transitions cannot be reconstructed. This is not a durable consent ledger. The interface does not present a legal consent form and does not process real consent.

Benefit records are hypothetical commitments only. Examples include sharing results in an accessible form, training community researchers, paying contributors where an arrangement is actually agreed, or offering useful tools. Each record should identify an accountable fictional owner, due date, evidence and review state. Empty evidence means delivery is unverified; no payment, revenue or delivered benefit is claimed.

Before real use, co-design understandable choices, withdrawal handling, accessible communication, grievance and remedy pathways, and fair compensation with affected people. Obtain legal and ethics review for the specific study and jurisdiction.

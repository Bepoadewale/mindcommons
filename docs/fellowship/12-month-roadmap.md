# A possible 12-month plan

This is a proposal for June 2027 to May 2028, if the fellowship is awarded and an authorized host relationship is established. It is not an SRHIN commitment. The work should change in response to people affected.

| Period                                       | Possible work                                                                                                                                                         | What could be shared or used to decide whether to continue                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Months 1–3: Listen and define the problem    | Work with the host, young people, people with lived experience and community health workers to learn which problems matter and what safe participation requires.      | A community-shaped problem statement, governance options, language and accessibility needs, and ethics/privacy questions. No health information is collected before approvals. |
| Months 4–6: Co-design and improve            | Explore different ways to explain permission, record decisions, handle disagreement and set benefit terms. Improve the software only where the co-design supports it. | Updated prototype, plain-language materials, tests, and a record of choices and open questions. Participants can reject the approach.                                          |
| Months 7–9: Prepare a small, controlled test | First agree safeguards, legal and ethics approvals, data handling, stop rules and host/community permission. Only then consider a tightly limited pilot.              | A written go/no-go decision. If a pilot is not safe or approved, use fictional examples and tabletop exercises instead.                                                        |
| Months 10–12: Learn and plan for maintenance | Review whether people understood and could influence the decisions, permission changes and benefit process. Plan who will maintain the open-source project.           | Findings approved for sharing, a maintenance plan, and a clear account of limits or negative results.                                                                          |

A successful project is not measured only by model accuracy. People affected should help define what agency, understanding, safety and accountability mean. Do not present these goals as results already achieved.
